# QA skill

Separate two forms of evidence:

1. **Visual critique** — comparative/artistic judgment from renders or viewport captures.
2. **Technical validation** — deterministic scene and mesh measurements.

A pass requires both where applicable.

Every failure must become a correction ticket containing:
- finding;
- evidence;
- owner;
- required change;
- acceptance check.

Do not close a ticket based only on the Builder saying it was fixed. Re-run the relevant check.
