# Animation skill

- Derive motion requirements from the brief and plan.
- Validate timing, spacing, arcs, weight, contact, penetration, and physical plausibility.
- For deformation, check topology and rig behavior at motion extremes.
- For rigid/physics motion, verify scale, mass assumptions, collision shapes, solver settings, and repeatability.
- Capture representative frames or playblasts for Critic.
- Keep actions, frame ranges, and naming explicit for export.
